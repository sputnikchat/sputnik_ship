What we collect

Sputnik Ship is designed to work with the minimum data needed: a username (handle) and password. We do not require your real name, email address, or government ID to create an account.

Shipment & contact data

Any shipment tracking numbers, carrier info, and contact details you add are stored to provide the service and are visible only to you and anyone you explicitly share a shipment with.

Photos

Photos you upload have their metadata (EXIF data, including location) automatically stripped before storage.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Settings.
